\chapter*{Acknowledgements}

First and foremost, I would like to thank my co-supervisors, PD Dr.\ Luis
Ter\'an and Prof.\ Edy Portmann, for their wise patience and steady
guidance over all these years. Their academic rigour, their generosity in
sharing ideas, and their unwavering trust in this project --- even
through its hardest stretches --- shaped both this thesis and the way I
think as a researcher. I am deeply grateful to both of you.

I am especially indebted to Dr.\ Fuad Huam\'an, histopathologist at SOLCA Guayaquil
(Ecuador), for his expert advice, his dedication, and his careful
feedback. His clinical perspective grounded the computational
contributions of this thesis in real diagnostic practice and prevented
the work from drifting away from what truly matters at the bedside.

My sincere gratitude goes to the SOLCA Guayaquil --- CISOL team in Ecuador for
their continuous support throughout this research: to Dr.\ Katherine
Garc\'ia (oncologist), Dr.\ Juan Carlos Ruiz (molecular biologist),
Dr.\ Marlon Pozo (histopathologist), and the many other specialists at
CISOL whose questions, data, and willingness to engage with an
interdisciplinary computer-science project enriched every chapter that
follows.

I am also profoundly grateful to Prof.\ Alessandra Curioni-Fontecedro and
her team at H\^opital fribourgeois (HFR) for their expert guidance and
for seeing the real clinical value of this research from very early on.
Their commitment to translational oncology has been a constant source
of motivation.

I owe a particular debt of gratitude to Prof.\ Jos\'e Mar\'ia Alonso
Moral (CiTIUS, Universidad de Santiago de Compostela), whose expertise
in interpretable and explainable fuzzy systems sits at the very heart of
this thesis. His careful reading and his thoughtful, demanding remarks
elevated the methodological and conceptual quality of this work in ways
that I could not have reached on my own.

Finally, my thanks extend to the many other specialists in computer
science and in medicine who, in conversations, reviews, and small but
decisive remarks, made this work possible. Any merit in the pages that
follow is shared with all of you; the remaining shortcomings are mine
alone.
